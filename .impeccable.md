# Design Context — Meme Accounts

## Target audience
Creators, solo marketers, and small agencies who ship social carousels, reels, and scheduled posts daily. They work fast, care about craft, and want tools that feel sharp rather than corporate SaaS.

## Use cases
- Compare Free / Pro / Agency and upgrade with confidence
- Complete a secure Stripe checkout without leaving brand trust
- Manage or cancel subscription from Settings → Billing

## Brand personality
Bold, creator-first, kinetic. Lime punch (#E8FF48) on near-black (#080808) with orange (#FF6B35) accent. Satoshi typography. Light marketing surfaces, dark product chrome. Confident, not cute; precise, not sterile.
