# Brand Carousel — slide story (API + UI)

When **slide count = 7** (default), aim for this **arc** (labels match the filmstrip):

1. **Hero** — Hook + promise; strongest typography; optional hero `data-img-slot`.
2. **Problem** — Tension, myth, or mistake the reader makes.
3. **Pivot** — Reframe: what actually matters / the insight.
4. **Logic #1** — First proof, mechanism, or step.
5. **Logic #2** — Second proof, nuance, or consequence.
6. **Pro Tip** — One actionable takeaway (boxed or highlighted, still tasteful).
7. **CTA** — Clear next step + handle; brand accent, not neon soup.

For **3–10** slides, compress or expand the arc: always keep **Hero** first and **CTA** last; merge “logic” beats if fewer slides; add depth if more.
